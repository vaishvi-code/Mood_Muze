Our Story

Music that understands you

Mood Muze redefines the music listening experience by bridging the gap between how you feel and what you hear — using real-time AI emotion recognition and your physical location to surface the perfect Spotify playlist every single time.

🎭

Emotion Recognition

Our browser-based AI uses your webcam to detect your facial expression in real time. It classifies your mood — happy, sad, angry, neutral, surprised and more — then instantly surfaces a Spotify playlist handpicked for that emotion.

🗺️

Location-Based Music

Share your location and Mood Muze reverse-geocodes it to your Indian state, then serves a curated playlist rooted in the music, culture, and flavour of where you are right now. 17 Indian states supported!

🔒

Private by Design

All emotion detection happens entirely in your browser — no video is ever sent to a server. Location data is only used for the geocoding lookup and is never stored. Your data stays yours.

🎵

Spotify Integration

Mood Muze plugs directly into Spotify's embed player — no login required. Every recommendation opens as an immersive in-page player so you can start listening instantly without leaving the app.
